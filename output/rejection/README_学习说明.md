## 01. Well, nobody likes rejection. But I think as you get older, you get used to it. And probably it's quite a good thing to experience rejection, even if it doesn't feel nice. Yeah, I agree. We can't always get what we want, can we?

- 中文翻译：嗯，没有人喜欢被拒绝。但是我觉得随着年龄的增长，你会渐渐习惯了。而且经历被拒绝可能还是件好事，哪怕感觉不舒服。是啊，我同意。我们不可能总是得到我们想要的，不是吗？
- Key expressions:
  - get used to it：逐渐习惯某事 表达某人慢慢适应了某种情况或事物 例句：After moving to the city, she got used to it pretty quickly.
  - it's quite a good thing to experience rejection：体验被拒绝其实是件好事 用于表达某事虽然不愉快，但对成长有积极意义 例句：Sometimes, it's quite a good thing to experience rejection because it makes you stronger.
  - We can't always get what we want：我们不可能总是得到我们想要的 用来表明生活中总会有挫折或不能如愿的时候 例句：We can't always get what we want, but we can try our best.
- Pronunciation notes:
  - 思考组：可按语意分组朗读，如 Well, nobody likes rejection / But I think as you get older, you get used to it / And probably it's quite a good thing to experience rejection, even if it doesn't feel nice / Yeah, I agree / We can't always get what we want, can we?
  - 重读信息词：可以强调 LIKE（likes rejection）、OLDER（get older）、USED（get used to it）、GOOD THING（quite a good thing）、REJECTION（experience rejection）、AGREE（I agree）、WANT（get what we want）等内容词，帮助表达重点。
  - 功能词弱读：'to' 在 'get used to it' 和 'good thing to experience rejection' 这两个短语中可以轻读成弱音，更加自然。

- 中文翻译：实际上，当我走向第一个人的时候，我感觉我的肾上腺素真的飙升了。我并不是一个有社交焦虑的人，我觉得我很自信。但第一次真的让我害怕。不过当我做过一次后，第二次就容易了，第三次更容易了。到第四次的时候，无论他们说同意还是不同意，我都无所谓了。
- Key expressions:
  - walking up to the first person：走向第一个人 用于描述主动接近某人，通常带有一定心理压力或勇气 例句：He was nervous about walking up to the first person at the networking event.
  - my adrenaline really, really spiking：我的肾上腺素真的飙升了 用来形容因为紧张、兴奋或害怕等情绪，身体反应非常强烈 例句：When I saw the roller coaster, my adrenaline really, really spiked.
  - once I'd done one, the second one came easier and the third one came even easier：当我做过一次后，第二次就容易了，第三次更容易了 用来表达随着经验积累，后面的事情变得越来越容易 例句：Once I'd given one speech, the next ones came easier and easier.
  - by the fourth time, I didn't even mind it whether they said yes or no：到第四次时，我根本不在乎他们是否同意 表达经过多次经历后，已经变得淡定，对结果无所谓 例句：By the fourth interview, I didn't even mind if I got the job or not.
- Pronunciation notes:
  - 使用思维组将句子分为：And actually walking up to the first person / I felt my adrenaline really, really spiking. / I'm not a socially anxious person. / I think I'm really confident. / But that first thing really scared me. / And once I'd done one / the second one came easier / and the third one came even easier. / And, you know, by the fourth time / I didn't even mind it whether they said yes or no.

  - 可以弱读 and、to 与 the等功能词。例如短语 “And once I'd done one”中的 and 可以轻读，练习节奏时尝试快速、轻柔地说出这些词，突出主要信息。

- 中文翻译：我们已经知道的是，完美主义在增加。现在人们、尤其是年轻人，对他人的评价更加担心。所以我在想，这是否会让人们变得更害羞，不愿意和陌生人说话，担心别人不会用积极的眼光看待自己。因此，做类似这样的事情来尝试对抗这种情况，可能会让人觉得自己正在积极地做点什么。此外，这些活动也很有趣，值得一看。
- Key expressions:
  - what we do know is that：我们确实知道的是 用于强调确定的事实，常用于引出确切的信息。 例句：What we do know is that the team will arrive tomorrow.
  - shy of speaking to strangers：害羞、不愿意和陌生人说话 用于描述因为某些原因而变得不敢与不认识的人交流。 例句：Some students are shy of speaking to strangers at first.
  - counter that：对抗那种情况 用于表示采取行动来对抗一个不理想的现象或趋势。 例句：He exercised more to counter that unhealthy lifestyle.
  - appraise them in a good way：用积极的方式评价他们 描述希望别人以好的、正面的角度来看待自己。 例句：I hope my manager will appraise me in a good way during the review.
- Pronunciation notes:
  - 注意思考组划分：建议这样分组朗读：What we do know is that / there is an increase in perfectionism. / People are more worried now, younger people, / about how other people are judging them. / And so I wonder whether that might make people more shy of speaking to strangers, / worried that the people will not appraise them in a good way. / And so doing something like this to try to counter that / might feel like a positive step to people that they're trying to do something. / Also, they're fun to watch.
  - 在强调性词语如 'INCREASE', 'PERFECTIONISM', 'WORRIED', 'JUDGING', 'SHY', 'APPRAISE', 'POSITIVE', 'FUN' 上加重读，帮助突出句子的主旨信息。
  - 可以尝试将 'and', 'to', 'that', 'they're' 这些功能词弱读，如 'and so', 'to try to counter that', 'they're trying to do something'，让句子节奏更自然、流畅。
