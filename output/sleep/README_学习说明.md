# Shadowing 学习说明

发音提示是基于文本的练习建议，请结合原音频模仿实际重音、停顿和语调。

这个学习包用于英语跟读练习。建议每个句子按以下节奏练习：先听原速，再看文本跟读，再听 0.8x 慢速，最后脱稿复述。

## 01. Now, Phil, do you ever struggle to get a good night's sleep? You know, I do sometimes. If I've been thinking about something a lot or even worse, if I've had a cup of coffee later than I should, sometimes I can lie there for hours. Yes. Well, many of us will be familiar with that feeling of lying in bed, feeling really tired, but just not being able to go to sleep. But what causes us to struggle to sleep? And is there anything we can do about it?

- Slow audio: `clips_0.8x/01_now_phil_do_you_ever_struggle_0.8x.mp3`
- 中文翻译：现在，Phil，你有没有过很难获得良好睡眠的时候？你知道吗，我有时会这样。如果我最近一直在想某件事，或者更糟糕的是，如果我喝了比应喝时间更晚的咖啡，我有时会躺在床上几个小时都睡不着。是的，我们很多人对那种躺在床上、感到非常疲倦却无法入睡的感觉并不陌生。那么，是什么让我们难以入睡呢？我们有没有什么办法可以解决这个问题？
- Key expressions:
  - struggle to get a good night's sleep：很难获得高质量的睡眠 表示在入睡或维持良好睡眠方面有困难 例句：After working late, I struggle to get a good night's sleep.
  - lie there for hours：在床上躺好几个小时 形容长时间躺在床上无法入睡的状态 例句：When I'm stressed, I can lie there for hours before falling asleep.
  - familiar with that feeling：对那种感觉很熟悉 表达大家都经历过类似的情绪或感受 例句：Many people are familiar with that feeling of nervousness before an interview.
  - is there anything we can do about it：我们有什么方法可以解决这件事吗 用来询问是否有办法应对某个问题 例句：I'm always so tired— is there anything we can do about it?
- Pronunciation notes:
  - 练习意群分组：可以这样分组朗读——Now, Phil, / do you ever struggle to get a good night's sleep? / You know, I do sometimes. / If I've been thinking about something a lot / or even worse, / if I've had a cup of coffee later than I should, / sometimes I can lie there for hours. / Yes. / Well, many of us will be familiar with that feeling of lying in bed, / feeling really tired, / but just not being able to go to sleep. / But what causes us to struggle to sleep? / And is there anything we can do about it?
  - 重读内容词：着重强调sleep, thinking, coffee, hours, feeling, bed, tired, sleep, causes, struggle, anything。比如：struggle to GET a GOOD NIGHT'S SLEEP；THINKING about SOMETHING a LOT。
  - the和a可以弱读成/ðə/ 和/ə/（如：a good night's sleep, the feeling），使语句更自然流畅。
- Shadowing tips: Listen once, repeat three times, then record yourself once.

## 02. Georgie, you've been looking at the research on this. Do scientists think that what we eat affects how we sleep? Yes, they do. Scientists have done some long -term studies, so looking at large groups of people over a long period of time. And they found that a healthier diet is associated with better quality sleep. They've also done some smaller studies, looking in more detail at exactly what people are eating, and then studying how they sleep in a laboratory.

- Time: 01:15.22 - 01:42.64
- Audio: `clips/02_georgie_you_ve_been_looking_at.mp3`
- Slow audio: `clips_0.8x/02_georgie_you_ve_been_looking_at_0.8x.mp3`
- 中文翻译：Georgie，你一直在关注这方面的研究。科学家认为我们的饮食会影响睡眠吗？是的，他们认为会。科学家们进行了一些长期研究，就是在长时间内观察大量人群。他们发现更健康的饮食与更好的睡眠质量有关。他们还做了一些小型研究，更详细地观察人们究竟吃什么，然后在实验室研究他们的睡眠。
- Key expressions:
  - long-term studies：长期研究 指在较长一段时间内持续追踪、分析同一对象或群体。 例句：They have done some long-term studies to observe health effects.
  - is associated with：与......有关 用来描述两者之间存在某种关系，经常用于科学或正式语境。 例句：Regular exercise is associated with lower stress levels.
  - in more detail：更详细地 表示用更深入、更细致的方式去观察或讨论某事。 例句：Let's examine the results in more detail.
- Pronunciation notes:
  - 练习思路分组：可以这样断句——Georgie / you've been looking at the research on this. / Do scientists think that what we eat affects how we sleep? / Yes, they do. / Scientists have done some long-term studies, so looking at large groups of people over a long period of time. / And they found that a healthier diet is associated with better quality sleep. / They've also done some smaller studies, looking in more detail at exactly what people are eating, / and then studying how they sleep in a laboratory.
  - 内容词重音：注意在LONG-term STUdies, large GROUPS of PEOple, HEALTHIER DIET, BETTER quality SLEEP等词组中，将大写的内容词读得更响亮清楚。
  - 弱读建议：'of' 在large groups of people和quality of sleep等短语中可以弱化，读得更轻微，使句子更自然。
- Shadowing tips: Listen once, repeat three times, then record yourself once.

## 03. OK, in these studies, have they found that particular foods are better for our sleep than others? Well, it's less about particular foods and more about particular nutrients. So nutrients means important things your body needs to survive and be healthy. For example, protein is a nutrient.

- Time: 01:43.22 - 02:02.20
- Audio: `clips/03_ok_in_these_studies_have_they.mp3`
- Slow audio: `clips_0.8x/03_ok_in_these_studies_have_they_0.8x.mp3`
- 中文翻译：好的，在这些研究中，他们有发现某些特定食物比其他食物更有助于我们的睡眠吗？其实，与其说是特定食物，倒不如说是特定营养素。营养素指的是你身体为生存和健康所需的重要东西。例如，蛋白质是营养素之一。
- Key expressions:
  - have they found that：他们有发现……吗 用于提出关于是否有发现新事实或结果的疑问。 例句：Have they found that this method is effective?
  - it's less about particular foods and more about particular nutrients：这更少是关于特定的食物，而更多关乎特定的营养素 用于比较两个方面，强调后者更为重要。 例句：It's less about winning and more about having fun.
  - nutrients means important things your body needs to survive and be healthy：营养素指的是你的身体为了生存和健康所需的重要东西 在讲解“nutrients”这个词时，解释其定义和重要性。 例句：Exercise means activities you do to stay healthy.
- Pronunciation notes:
  - 建议分组朗读：OK / in these studies / have they found that particular foods are better for our sleep than others? / Well / it's less about particular foods and more about particular nutrients. / So nutrients means important things your body needs to survive and be healthy. / For example / protein is a nutrient.
  - 要在主要内容词如：studies, found, particular foods, sleep, nutrients, important, needs, survive, healthy, protein 上做重读，突出信息。
  - 像“have they”、“and”、“to”在句中可以弱读，例如“and”在‘and more about particular nutrients’中可轻读 [ənd]，让语流更自然。
