# Shadowing 学习说明

发音提示是基于文本的练习建议，请结合原音频模仿实际重音、停顿和语调。

这个学习包用于英语跟读练习。建议每个句子按以下节奏练习：先听原速，再看文本跟读，再听 0.8x 慢速，最后脱稿复述。

## 01. So, how do scientists calculate the effects of climate change in the first place? The answer is with models. Huge amounts of physical and chemical data are fed into supercomputers, which then paint pictures of possible futures on Earth. But as NASA climate scientist Kate Marvel explained to BBC World Service programme, the climate question, nothing is certain.

- Audio: `clips/01_so_how_do_scientists_calculate_the.mp3`
- Slow audio: `clips_0.8x/01_so_how_do_scientists_calculate_the_0.8x.mp3`
- 中文翻译：那么，科学家一开始是如何计算气候变化的影响的呢？答案是通过模型。大量物理和化学数据被输入超级计算机，然后这些计算机会描绘出地球可能的未来图景。但正如美国宇航局气候科学家凯特·马弗尔在BBC世界服务节目《气候问题》中解释的那样，没有什么是确定的。
- Key expressions:
  - in the first place：首先，起初 用于强调某事的起始原因或本质原因，经常用于提问或者解释动机。 例句：Why did you agree to the plan in the first place?
  - paint pictures of possible futures：描绘可能的未来场景 多用于形容用描述或数据预测不同结果或者可能出现的情况。 例句：Economists try to paint pictures of possible futures based on current trends.
  - nothing is certain：没有什么是确定的，一切都不确定 用来表达未知性或无法把握的情况，常见于科学、预测等讨论。 例句：In science, nothing is certain until it is proved again and again.
- Pronunciation notes:
  - 将句子分为思维组：So, how do scientists calculate the effects of climate change in the first place? / The answer is with models. / Huge amounts of physical and chemical data are fed into supercomputers, / which then paint pictures of possible futures on Earth. / But as NASA climate scientist Kate Marvel explained / to BBC World Service programme, the climate question, / nothing is certain.
  - 在内容词上加强重读，例如：SCI-entists, CAL-cu-late, EF-fects, CLI-mate CHANGE, HUGE a-MOUNTS, PHYS-i-cal, CHEM-i-cal, SUPER-computers, PAINT PICTURES, POSSIBLE FUTURES, EARTH, NOTHING, CERTAIN。
  - 像“of”这种功能词在句中可弱化为弱读，读作类似/əv/，如 the effects of climate change, pictures of possible futures。

## 02. Climate models don't make predictions, those are statements about what's likely to happen. Instead they make projections about different possible futures. And these depend on many things. In other words, climate models are conditional, meaning that they depend on other factors. The main factor in climate change, of course, is human activity, especially greenhouse gas emissions.

- Time: 02:18.38 - 02:43.12
- Audio: `clips/02_climate_models_don_t_make_predictions.mp3`
- Slow audio: `clips_0.8x/02_climate_models_don_t_make_predictions_0.8x.mp3`
- 中文翻译：气候模型并不是做预测，那些是关于未来可能发生什么的判断。相反，它们会对不同的可能未来做出推断。而这些推断取决于很多因素。换句话说，气候模型是有条件的，这意味着它们依赖于其他因素。当然，气候变化的主要因素是人类活动，尤其是温室气体排放。
- Key expressions:
  - don't make predictions：并不是做预测 用于强调某事不是人们通常以为的功能或内容。 例句：Smartphones don't make predictions; they just follow user input.
  - In other words：换句话说 引出对前文内容的进一步解释或重新表达。 例句：He's frugal. In other words, he doesn't like to spend money unnecessarily.
  - depend on：取决于，依赖于 用于说明某事物发生或存在依赖于另一事物。 例句：Success in this exam will depend on your preparation.
  - The main factor：主要因素 用于强调某事件、结果的最重要的原因或组成部分。 例句：The main factor in her recovery was early treatment.
- Pronunciation notes:
  - 练习意群划分：把句子分为自然的思考单元，如：Climate models don't make predictions / those are statements about what's likely to happen. / Instead they make projections about different possible futures. / And these depend on many things. / In other words, climate models are conditional / meaning that they depend on other factors. / The main factor in climate change, of course, is human activity / especially greenhouse gas emissions.
  - 重读内容词：在“PROJECTIONS about DIFFERENT POSSIBLE FUTURES”和“The MAIN FACTOR in CLIMATE CHANGE”中，重读 projects、different、possible、factor、climate、change等主旨词。
  - 连读弱读：像“and these depend on many things.”、“in other words”，可以弱读 and 和 in，使句子更自然。例如，可以把and弱化为/ənd/，而不是/ænd/，以增强英语节奏感。
- Shadowing tips: Listen once, repeat three times, then record yourself once.

## 03. So the thing about clouds is that they have this dual effect. They both cool the planet by blocking sunlight and they warm the planet because they have their own greenhouse effect. Right now the unit that scientists use to measure the energy imbalance of the planet, how much it's out of whack, is watts like in a light bulb per square meter.

- Time: 02:57.16 - 03:19.08
- Audio: `clips/03_so_the_thing_about_clouds_is.mp3`
- Slow audio: `clips_0.8x/03_so_the_thing_about_clouds_is_0.8x.mp3`
- 中文翻译：云的特点在于它们有双重作用。它们一方面通过遮挡阳光来让地球降温，另一方面又因为自身的温室效应使地球变暖。现在，科学家们用的衡量地球能量失衡的单位是瓦特，就像灯泡的瓦特，每平方米这么算。
- Key expressions:
  - dual effect：双重作用；两个相反或并存的影响 用于描述某事物同时具有两种不同或相反的效果。 例句：Exercise can have a dual effect: it relieves stress but can also tire you out.
  - out of whack：失衡；异常；不正常 用于表示某事物不再按正常情况运转或有问题。 例句：My computer's calendar is out of whack, so all the reminders are messed up.
  - per square meter：每平方米 用于表明某个数值是在每平方米的范围内计算的。 例句：The rent is $20 per square meter.
- Pronunciation notes:
  - 练习断句时，可以这样分组：So the thing about clouds is / that they have this dual effect. / They both cool the planet / by blocking sunlight / and they warm the planet / because they have their own greenhouse effect. / Right now / the unit that scientists use / to measure the energy imbalance of the planet, / how much it's out of whack, / is watts like in a light bulb per square meter.
  - 重读内容词，比如“DUAL effect”，“COOL the PLANET”，“WARM the PLANET”，“GREENHOUSE effect”，“ENERGY IMBALANCE”，“WATTS”，“LIGHT bulb”，“SQUARE meter”，让这些核心词更突出。
  - “and”和“of”可以弱化为 /ənd/ 或 /əv/，如 “by blocking sunlight and they warm the planet” 里的“and”，还有 “energy imbalance of the planet” 里的“of”，可以变得很弱，节奏更自然。

- 中文翻译：嗯，皮帕，气候模型听起来对我来说非常复杂。首先，你怎么能监测那些不断变化和移动的云呢？是啊，还有，超级计算机要从世界各地收集多少信息，才能做出准确的预测？这是BBC主持人格蕾雅·杰克逊在《BBC世界服务》节目《气候问题》中向凯特·马维尔提出的一个担忧。当然，我们肯定需要了解我们正朝着哪个方向前进，以便做出适应。哦，当然。你知道，我非常支持科学。我非常支持做科学研究，因为很不幸，气候变化不是一个可以忽略的问题。
- Key expressions:
  - for one thing：首先；一方面 用来在列举理由或观点时，强调第一个要点。 例句：For one thing, it’s too expensive. For another, it’s not reliable.
  - monitor clouds which are constantly moving and changing：监测那些不断移动和变化的云 用来描述难以追踪或观察的事情。 例句：It’s difficult to monitor clouds which are constantly moving and changing.
  - in order to adapt：为了适应 强调做某事的目的，用来解释为什么需要某样东西或采取措施。 例句：We need to change our habits in order to adapt to the new climate.
  - not on the table：不可选择；不在考虑范围内 指某个选项或建议现在不被考虑或不可能实现。 例句：Moving to another country is not on the table right now.
- Pronunciation notes:
  - 将长句拆分思路组，比如：Hmm / climate models SOUND very COMPLICATED to me, Pippa. / For one thing / how can you MONITOR CLOUDS which are CONSTANTLY MOVING and CHANGING? /
  - 强调内容词，如 'COMPLICATED', 'MONITOR', 'SUPERCOMPUTERS', 'INFORMATION', 'PROJECTIONS', 'TRAJECTORY', 'ADAPT' 等，要读得更有力、拖长时间。
  - 常用功能词如 'and', 'to', 'of' 可以弱读。例如 'in order TO adapt' 里 ‘to’ 可轻而快带过，保持句子节奏自然。
